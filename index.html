Important Notice & Privacy Disclaimer

Welcome to Student Hub! Before you log in, please read and acknowledge the following:

1. Unofficial Application Student Hub is an unofficial, community-built alternative interface. It is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, associated with, or a mirror of any official college portal.
2. Data Privacy & Local Storage We value your privacy and design with minimal data retention in mind:
  • Local Credentials: Your login details are stored locally in your browser's storage and used to fetch your attendance and CIE records.
  • Leaderboard Results: Publicly published semester exam results and SGPA scores are stored securely in the database to generate the Branch Leaderboard.
  • No Selling of Data: Your data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for tracking outside of Student Hub features.
3. Photo Caching To reduce server load on the official portal, we can fetch your profile photo once and cache it.
4. User Consent & Risk By proceeding, you acknowledge that you are willingly providing your credentials to be used locally by this application, and you agree to use this service at your own risk.
