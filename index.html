Privacy Policy & Terms

Welcome to Student Hub. We are committed to transparency regarding how your data is handled.

1. Local Storage of Credentials
  • Your credentials (USN, DOB, verification code, and Moodle login) are stored strictly in your browser's local storage.
  • We do not retain your passwords or personal portal credentials on external databases.
2. Direct Portal Communication
  • All attendance, CIE, and Moodle requests are proxied directly to the official NIE college servers.
  • No scraping logic or private data is exposed publicly.
